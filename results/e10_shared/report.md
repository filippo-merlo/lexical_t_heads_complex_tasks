# E10: one shared 30-head set for all tasks, from E8's five scores

Each of E8's per-head scores (D decoding, B BOS, T task words, I input, R rest; choosing half) is combined over the 14 tasks: `mean` (average), `worst` (weakest task), `rank` (average within-task rank); higher is better for D, T, I, lower for B, R. For each of the 31 combinations the heads are ranked on each of its filters' combined scores, the ranks averaged, and the best 30 kept: one set for every task (each task still patched with its own means). Accuracy at the cross-fitted alpha (1 or 2), mean of the two folds, mean over the 14 tasks. `e8` = E8's per-task set of the same combination (its thresholds, each task its own heads and size).
